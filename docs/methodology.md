# Data methodology

## Observations and forecasts

| Feed | What SaansAI uses | Availability and limits |
|---|---|---|
| CPCB via data.gov.in and India API | Nearest eligible station in the relay's five-station response within 25 km. Displays the relay's India-scale station AQI and PM2.5 / PM10 sub-indices, the station name, distance and upstream timestamp. | The relay documents hourly refresh, anonymous requests at 600 units/min, and 3,000 units/min with a free key. Fresh relay observations and authenticated OpenAQ readings were verified across a six-city production sample on 26 November 2026. See the evaluation report for which source was selected in each city. Coverage and recency depend on the station. |
| OpenAQ v3 | When configured and recent, shows latest PM2.5 and PM10 mass concentration with each sensor's timestamp. Reference stationary monitor search is limited to 25 km. | Requires an API key. Free limits are 60 requests/min and 2,000/hour per key. An unauthenticated API probe returned 401. Authenticated production responses provided measurements in the cities listed in the evaluation report. The public AWS archive is delayed by 72 hours after each local day ends. |
| Open-Meteo Air Quality / CAMS | The latest model estimate, hourly PM2.5 and PM10 in µg/m³, and US AQI. India uses the global CAMS model at roughly 45 km resolution. | Public non-commercial tier. India-hour bins are presented in IST. Forecast data may lag or differ from street-level measurements. |
| Open-Meteo Weather and GeoNames | Temperature, humidity and wind estimates. City and postcode search returns only India-tagged results. | Models and stations can be incomplete. Weather can fail independently of the AQI forecast. |

Relay pollutant values are sub-indices. SaansAI neither applies concentration units to them nor treats the relay's composite as CPCB's official city AQI. The station and model use separate cards and index labels. Both observation products can appear together, and both independently inform the caution rule when fresh. A fresh eligible relay report takes priority over a closer stale one. Missing values stay missing. Station readings over 3 hours old are stale. Values older than 48 hours or more than 5 minutes in the future are discarded. The nearest match is a city-reference monitor, with no street-level interpolation.

## Decisions

The planner discards expired or stale model data. A timed suggestion needs two consecutive future forecast hours in the next 24 hours that are wholly outside work hours and inside the app's 07:00–20:00 scheduling period. It minimizes the higher US AQI of the two hours, then selects the earlier tie. The ceiling is US AQI 50 for sensitive households, play and exercise and 100 for other errands. Current model AQI above 100 for sensitive households or 150 for others blocks a timed suggestion. A fresh conflicting station reading also blocks it. India-scale AQI or a PM sub-index at least 101, or OpenAQ PM2.5 at least 35.5 µg/m³ or PM10 at least 155 µg/m³, are conservative application screens. They do not derive a local AQI or assess safety. The app recommends checking local conditions and following official advisories.

Ventilation uses the current model, station conflict screen and modeled temperature of 18–32 °C as product criteria. The app has no indoor sensor, building, or emergency information. Source control and compatible exhaust or filtration are available independently of the outdoor suggestion. ## Estimates

- Petrol used per month = user-entered petrol km per changed day × changed days ÷ km/L.
- Tailpipe CO₂ = litres of petrol × 2.34 kg CO₂/L. This is a UK 2026 reporting proxy, not an India-specific factor. It excludes upstream fuel production, other gases, mode substitution emissions and vehicle lifecycle. [DUKES 2026, Annex A](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6a6a3b710c36759b5ccaa1e5/DUKES_2026_Annexes_A-J.pdf)
- Fuel and fare savings = difference in fuel use × user-entered price − alternative daily spend × changed days. Maintenance, parking and depreciation are excluded. Costs may increase.
- Community result = the signed per-household tailpipe change × the illustrative number of households. It is neither participation tracking nor a measured emissions reduction.
- Outdoor PM2.5 proxy = hourly modeled concentration converted to µg·h/m³ by multiplying by outdoor minutes ÷ 60. Each duration stays within its selected one-hour bin. The result describes ambient concentration-time and excludes route, indoor infiltration, ventilation rate and inhaled dose.

## Assistant boundary

Gemini receives one short question, response language, a household sensitivity flag, commute and activity type, an outdoor-work flag, two separately named AQI fields and the relevant caution and schedule eligibility flags. The assistant chooses one or two validated topic IDs. The app generates wording, numeric values and source links from its guidance catalog and rules. Prompts do not include coordinates, work hours, budget or a persistent account identifier. Common contact details detected in the question keep it in Rules mode. This check cannot detect every form of personal information. Avoid including your name, address, contact details or medical information.

On the Render backend, identical context and questions share a successful classification for five minutes. The in-memory key is a hash. The answer is recomposed from the current environmental data and profile. A timeout, 429, exhausted app budget or malformed model output can trigger an explicit Rules response. Call limits are per service process and can reset on restart. Provider limits are separately applied per Google project. [Gemini model](https://aistudio.google.com/docs/models/gemini-3.5-flash-lite) · [Gemini API rate limits](https://aistudio.google.com/docs/rate-limits)

In the keyless Pages build, city queries go from the browser to the public providers. Questions and household settings stay local. In production, the Pages frontend sends validated requests to Render. Household preferences and completed checklist items persist on the current browser. The app provides no account or cross-device sync.
